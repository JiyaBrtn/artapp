DISCOVER FILIPINO ART

Indigenous arts have greatly influenced Filipino life and culture. They are considered labors of love and patience because they require skill, creativity, and careful craftsmanship. Filipino tribes create intricate carvings using wood, metal, stone, and glass, as well as musical instruments made from bamboo, animal skin, seeds, and seashells. These instruments are commonly used in tribal dances and rituals. These traditional arts and crafts represent Filipino creativity, culture, and heritage that we should be proud of.

    05 / KEEPERS OF TRADITION

    GAMABA AWARDS AND
    QUALIFICATIONS

    In April 1992, the Gawad sa Manlilikha ng Bayan, or National Living Treasures Award, was institutionalized through Republic Act No. 7355. The National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) administers and implements the award.

    Through the Gawad sa Manlilikha ng Bayan Executive Council, the NCCA searches for the finest traditional artists, supports the transfer of their skills, and promotes appreciation and pride in the genius of the Manlilikha ng Bayan.

    QUALIFICATIONS

    1. Membership in an indigenous or traditional cultural community.
    2. A documented folk art tradition existing for at least 50 years.
    3. Consistent production or performance of superior-quality works.
    4. Mastery of tools and materials.
    5. An established reputation as a master practitioner.
    6. Ability and willingness to pass skills to the community.

    07 / THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PROJECT

    MEET THE CREATORS

    As a group, we learned that Filipino indigenous arts and crafts reflect our history, traditions, beliefs, and creativity. They represent Filipino identity through unique designs, dances, music, and handmade crafts.

    Preserving these traditions is important because they carry the knowledge and stories of our ancestors. As young people, we can support traditional artists by promoting their work, purchasing authentic crafts, and sharing their cultural significance.

    WITH APPRECIATION

    We acknowledge the artists, indigenous communities, craftspeople, photographers, and image makers whose work celebrates Filipino heritage. Games and site interactions support learning and discovery. This collection was created by BSCS students of Surigao del Norte State University.
